## Claims dropped (no official source opened, no year, or source blocked)

- Incapacity benefit amounts (82,64 / 65,56 / 50,28 / 56,21 euro per day): RSVZ page gives no year.
- Thresholds of the RSVZ pages without a stated year were used only where the 2026 PDF publishes the same figure.
- Primostarter, artikel 37, student-zelfstandige and pensioner contribution rules: in the PDF, but left out to keep the pages to the requested topics (only named).
- Distinction between "winst" and "baten" (arts. 24 and 27 WIB 92): the Fisconet articles could not be opened in this session; not explained.
- Voorafbetalingen (advance tax payments) of self-employed, forfaitaire beroepskosten, tax rates applied to self-employed income: FOD Financiën website behind CAPTCHA; not covered.
- Inkomstenjaar 2026 = aanslagjaar 2027 for self-employed income: not opened in this session for this section; only linked to the glossary.
- VLAIO information on starting a business (KBO, ondernemingsloket): www.vlaio.be refused automated access (403); only named as "not covered".
- Minimum pension, pension amounts, maternity benefits: not covered.
