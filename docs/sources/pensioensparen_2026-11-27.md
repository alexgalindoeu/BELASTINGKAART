# Pensioensparen / épargne-pension and langetermijnsparen / épargne à long terme, income year 2026 (assessment year 2027)

- **Source:** FOD Financiën / SPF Finances, Fisconetplus. Codified WIB 92 / CIR 92 articles in their "inkomsten 2026 / revenus 2026" version, the Wetboek diverse rechten en taksen / Code des droits et taxes divers (Book II, Title VIII), the FOD notice "Automatische indexering – Inkomsten 2026 – Aanslagjaar 2027", circular 2018/C/72 and one parliamentary question (only for the name "anticipatieve heffing").
- **Consulted:** 27-11-2026, through the public endpoint `https://www.minfin.fgov.be/myminfin-rest/fisconetPlus/public/document/<guid>` (no CAPTCHA; finances.belgium.be was not used). Human-readable link: `https://www.minfin.fgov.be/myminfin-web/pages/public/fisconet/document/<guid>`. A first reading was done on 26-11-2026 through the Fisconetplus web interface; every quote below was checked again against the 27-11-2026 copy.
- **Jurisdiction:** federal personal income tax (WIB 92) and federal tax on long-term savings; applies in all three regions. Income year 2026, assessment year 2027. Amounts are the indexed amounts printed in the articles ("geïndexeerd bedrag" / "montant indexé"), confirmed by the indexation notice. Unit: euro per taxable period and per taxpayer who holds the contract. No rounding is involved: the pages show only the legal ceilings and rates, never a computed amount.
- **Transcription:** verbatim, whitespace normalised; superscripts of article numbers written as "145/8". The circular 2018/C/72 is quoted for the mechanism of the choice; its euro amounts are the 2018 base amounts (625 / 800) and its examples use assessment year 2019 amounts, so no figure is taken from it.
- **Not stated by these sources, therefore not on the pages:** the term "taxe anticipative" (no official French source found; the French pages use "taxe sur l’épargne à long terme"), the tax treatment of payments made between 60 and 65, municipal surcharges on separately taxed pension savings, and any amount of tax for a given saver.
