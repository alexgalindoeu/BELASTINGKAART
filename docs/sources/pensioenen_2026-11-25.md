# Pensions section (nl/pensioenen/, fr/pensions/): claim → quote → source

- **Scope:** the Belgian legal pension as paid by the Federale Pensioendienst / Service fédéral des Pensions (FPD/SFP), the social withholdings on it, the professional withholding tax and the tax reduction of article 147 WIB 92 / CIR 92. Conceptual only: no personal amount is computed.
- **Consulted:** 25-11-2026 for every FPD/SFP page and for the Dutch text of article 147 WIB 92; 26-11-2026 for the French text of article 147 CIR 92 (the session crossed midnight). Each line below gives its own date; the pages print the same date per source.
- **Method:** FPD/SFP pages with plain HTTPS GET (HTTP 200, no CAPTCHA). Fisconetplus (`eservices.minfin.fgov.be`, redirected to `www.minfin.fgov.be/myminfin-web/pages/public/fisconet`) with Playwright: the cookie banner was refused, the public search was used, the public document was opened. No CAPTCHA was shown or bypassed on these hosts.
- **Not usable:** `financien.belgium.be` / `finances.belgium.be` and `www.belgium.be` returned an image CAPTCHA ("What code is in the image?") to both `curl` and Playwright on 25-11-2026. It was not bypassed and no statement relies on those sites. `www.mypension.be` is a JavaScript application without readable public text; it is linked as the place to consult one's own file, and every statement about it is sourced from the FPD page `mypension-be`.
- **Quotes** are verbatim, whitespace normalised. Generator: `tools/build_pensioenen.mjs`; tests: `test/pensioenen.test.mjs` (every source URL printed on the pages must appear below with its consultation date).

## Claims dropped for lack of a usable official source

- How the professional withholding on pensions is computed (scale, Annex III KB/WIB 92): the FPD links to `financien.belgium.be/.../bedrijfsvoorheffing/berekening`, blocked by a CAPTCHA.
- Further limits on the pension reduction in other articles of WIB 92 (e.g. articles 148 and following) and their interaction with article 147: not opened, so not described.
